# Privacy Policy for React Native Auth Libraries Example App

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026

**1. Our Commitment to Privacy**
At Example, we believe that your data is your own. Our privacy philosophy is simple: we do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information.

**2. Data Collection and Usage**

* **Zero Data Collection:** We do not collect any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location).
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics, tracking scripts, or crash reporting tools. Your usage behavior remains completely private and on your device.
* **No Advertising:** Example does not display targeted advertisements and does not share any data with advertising networks.

**3. Local Storage**
Any data generated by your use of the Example app is stored strictly locally on your device. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers.

**4. Changes to This Policy**
If we ever decide to change our privacy practices, we will update this document. However, our fundamental commitment to zero data collection will not change without your explicit consent.

**5. Contact Us**
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using [our contact page](https://treeentertainment.github.io/contact).
